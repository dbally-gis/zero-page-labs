The evidence

From plan to model.

The 3D scene uses approximate hull stations read from the half-breadth drawing and a deck arrangement interpreted from the inboard profile. The original drawings remain available in the museum catalogue.

  1. ZAZ1357 · Body, sheer and half-breadth ↗Admiralty drawing, c. 1758. Shared design for Bellona, Dragon, and Superb.
  2. ZAZ1359 · Inboard profile ↗Admiralty drawing, c. 1759. Use the original shared profile with care around later class alterations.
  3. Bellona · Construction model ↗Royal Museums Greenwich. Exposed framing and sections of planking.
  4. Falconer · Exercise of the great guns ↗Period gun drill used to reconstruct the repeated firing cycle.
  5. Seamanship · 1797 Encyclopædia Britannica ↗Period instructions for the order of a successful tack. The demonstration is representative.
