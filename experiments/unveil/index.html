About the pictures

Every scene is a real place, photographed from about 786 km up by the European Space Agency’s Sentinel-2 satellites. Each is a true-colour mosaic of the least cloudy passes over a season or a year. The satellites see about 10 metres to a pixel; the scenes here run from 6 to 200 km across, so most are scaled down.

A new scene comes up at your local midnight; everyone gets the same scene on the same date. Practice scenes are random and don’t count.

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2024–2025), processed through the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem. Notes on each place draw on the linked Wikipedia articles.
