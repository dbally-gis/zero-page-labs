WHAT THE CIRCLES SHOW

Same on Earth.
Different on the page.

Every blue circle has the same 5° angular radius on the sphere. Stretching reveals changes in shape; changes in size reveal area distortion. These are finite circles, not infinitesimal Tissot indicatrices.

WHAT YOU’RE SEEING

A transformation,
not a measurement.

This is the Projection cabinet’s map engine from Globe & Atlas: Orbits, with bundled Natural Earth coastlines. It runs locally in your browser. Intermediate unfolding is an illustrative deformation, not a physical projection construction.

Equal-area maps preserve area. Conformal maps preserve local angles. No flat world map preserves everything.
