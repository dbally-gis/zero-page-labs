How to read this reconstruction

Historical dates, participants and the broad sequence come from the sources below. Ship positions, headings, continuous tracks, smoke and timing are authored for explanation. This is a schematic replay, not a physics simulation or a measured navigational reconstruction.

Dimensional ship models illustrate generic vessel types; the coloured pennants are fleet keys rather than historical flags. Named ships identify selected participants. Numbered formation markers represent the wider fleet; they do not constitute a complete order of battle. Ships are enlarged for legibility. Coast and shoal shapes are illustrative, not navigational charts. Weapon sectors explain mounting geometry, not verified range or firing clearance. Trafalgar uses selected source-linked episodes: a rake, reciprocal broadsides and Lucas’s reported stern fire. Animated rounds, impact locations, fire onset and replay intervals are illustrative; they do not form a shot-by-shot log. Other encounters retain synthetic gunfire. No encounter calculates ship damage. Wind direction is illustrative; steam encounters omit it.

Replay progress is divided into five teaching chapters, without claiming exact historical times. The atlas does not calculate alternative outcomes. Use the linked sources to investigate further and compare accounts.
