These are four selected examples from European cartography, not a complete history of geographical error. A map’s precision of line is no guarantee of certainty. Nor does a European map’s blank space mean that a place was unknown to its inhabitants.

Each image is cropped or proportionally resized from the linked scan. No historical geography has been redrawn. “Whole sheet” shows the complete supplied digital image; the collection link leads to the original record. Dates mark particular maps or events, not a universal moment of correction.
