PROJECT 01 / WORD GAMES / IPHONE & IPAD

Thumbs, meet
your next puzzle.

Remember tapping 4-4-4 to get an I? There’s a whole new reason to pick up that keypad.

Thumbwords turns a familiar phone keypad into six word games. Decode clues, discover words hiding on the same keys, uncover phrases, or see how fast your thumbs can go.

Codebreaker

Three clues. Their keypad numbers. Find the words and the connection between them.

02

Number Hunt

One string of digits can hide a surprising number of words. See how many you can find.

03

Hidden Phrase

Reveal the keys behind a phrase, then call its letters to piece it together.

04

Sprint

Text like it’s 1999. Race through a phrase and chase your own personal best.

05

Textfall

Turn falling number blocks into words before the stack gets the better of you.

06

Key Ladder

Get from one word to another by changing one keypad digit at a time.
