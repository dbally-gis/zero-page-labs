EXPERIMENT 03 / A VISUAL LANGUAGE

Less surface.
More structure.

Gridcore is an experiment in making the underlying shape of things visible.

Developed as the visual language of Globe & Atlas, Gridcore is also at home here in the Labs: a specification to explore, test, and put to work.

Its starting point is simple. Strip away texture, lighting, and decoration until the geometry can speak for itself.

Read the specification ↗
