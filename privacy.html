Privacy

Updated November 25, 2026.

With your permission, Zero Page Labs uses Google Analytics to understand page views, visits, traffic sources, scrolling, outbound links, and file downloads. Google receives browser and device information and approximate location derived from your connection. Analytics cookies help distinguish visits. We do not enable advertising personalization.

Analytics loads only if you choose Allow analytics. Declining keeps Google Analytics off. Your choice is stored in this browser. Use Analytics settings at the bottom of any page to change it. If you withdraw permission, we remove this site's Google Analytics cookies and reload the page. This does not erase data previously received by Google.

We omit URL fragments and query parameters from page-view addresses except standard UTM campaign labels used to identify shared links. We do not track search terms, form interactions, or text entered into the experiments.

Read how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google's privacy policy.

The hosting provider processes requests to deliver the site regardless of your analytics choice.
